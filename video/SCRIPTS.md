# Munni's videos — what to film

Five short videos for the topics young children find hardest. Until a video is
filmed, the app plays an **animated version** of the same script: Munni's voice
over the app's own pictures. Once a clip is filmed and listed in
`video/clips.json`, the app plays the clip instead. The questions stay the same,
and so does how the child's answers are counted.

The point of filming is a real person with real things in their hands. Young
children learn more when they see something done than when it is only drawn.
That only holds if the video **stops and asks** them, so every script has two
pauses. When the teacher asks the question, the app freezes the clip and shows
two big answer buttons. The child taps one, and Munni says whether it's right.
Then the clip carries on and the teacher explains the answer, the same way
whichever button the child tapped.

| Video id | Topic | Classes | Skill | Length |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `v-tens` | Bundles of ten (tens and ones) | 1–3 | `place2` | ~2½ min |
| `v-carry` | Carrying a ten (38 + 24) | 2–3 | `carry` | ~2½ min |
| `v-borrow` | Borrowing a ten (52 − 27) | 2–3 | `borrow` | ~2½ min |
| `v-matra` | The ा and ि signs | UKG–1 | `u-matra1` | ~2 min |
| `v-clock` | What o'clock is it? | 1–2 | `time2` | ~2 min |

Film each video **twice: once in Hindi, once in English**. Don't mix the two
languages in one take. A language that isn't filmed keeps playing the animated
version.

---

## Before you film (all five)

- **Who:** one teacher, or a parent who explains things calmly. Film adults only, never a child's face.
- **Camera:** a phone held sideways (landscape) at the teacher's eye level, kept still (a tripod, or a pile of books). Film the things on the table close up.
- **Room:** quiet, with daylight from a window in front of the teacher, not behind. No fan noise, and no music anywhere in the video.
- **Speaking:** slowly, in short sentences, looking at the camera as if one child were sitting there. Smile.
- **Real things:** sticks or straws with rubber bands, a slate or a big sheet of paper with a thick marker, a real wall clock with movable hands.
- **The pauses:** ask the question, then **stop talking and look at the camera for 3 seconds**. Write down the second at which you finish asking, because the app stops there. Then carry on as the script says.
- **Never say "wrong"** in either language. The explanation after a pause is for every child.

## 1. `v-tens` — Bundles of ten

**Props:** 34 sticks (matchsticks, ice-cream sticks or straws) and 3 rubber bands.

| # | Show | Say (Hindi) | Say (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ten loose sticks, counted slowly | "दस तीलियाँ। एक-एक करके गिनने में बहुत समय लगता है!" | "Ten sticks. Counting them one by one takes so long!" |
| 2 | Tie the ten with a rubber band and hold the bundle up | "इसलिए हम दस तीलियों को एक साथ बाँध देते हैं। एक बंडल यानी एक दहाई।" | "So we tie ten sticks together. One bundle is one ten." |
| 3 | 2 bundles and 3 loose sticks | "दो बंडल और तीन खुली तीलियाँ: दो दहाई और तीन इकाई। यानी तेईस।" | "Two bundles and three loose sticks: two tens and three ones. That is twenty-three." |
| 4 | 3 bundles and 4 loose sticks on the table, close up | "इन तीलियों को देखो। दस के कितने बंडल हैं?" | "Look at these sticks. How many bundles of ten?" |
| ⏸ **Pause 1** | Look at the camera, 3 s | Answers on screen: **3**, **4** (right: 3) | |
| 5 | Touch each bundle | "बंडल गिनो: एक, दो, तीन। तीन दहाई। और चार खुली तीलियाँ — चार इकाई। चौंतीस।" | "Count the bundles: one, two, three. Three tens. And four loose sticks — four ones. Thirty-four." |
| 6 | Write **34** big; point to 3, then 4 | "34 में, 3 बताता है दस के बंडल, और 4 बताता है खुली तीलियाँ।" | "In 34, the 3 tells the bundles of ten, and the 4 tells the loose ones." |
| 7 | Hold up 5 fingers, then 2 | "अब तुम सोचो। पाँच दहाई और दो इकाई कौन सी संख्या है?" | "Now you think. Which number is five tens and two ones?" |
| ⏸ **Pause 2** | Look at the camera, 3 s | Answers: **25**, **52** (right: 52) | |
| 8 | Write **52** | "बावन: पहले 5 दहाई, फिर 2 इकाई। दहाई पहले आती है।" | "Fifty-two: 5 tens first, then 2 ones. The tens come first." |

## 2. `v-carry` — Carrying a ten

**Props:** a slate or a big sheet with **38 + 24** written as a column sum. Also 6 bundles of ten and 12 loose sticks, if you can.

| # | Show | Say (Hindi) | Say (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The column sum | "38 जमा 24। हम हमेशा इकाई से शुरू करते हैं।" | "38 plus 24. We always start with the ones." |
| 2 | Point to 8 and 4 | "8 इकाई और 4 इकाई मिलकर 12 इकाई। यह नौ से ज़्यादा है! 12 इकाई: इकाई के नीचे क्या लिखेंगे?" | "8 ones and 4 ones make 12 ones. That is more than nine! 12 ones: what do we write under the ones?" |
| ⏸ **Pause 1** | Look at the camera, 3 s | Answers: **12**, **2** (right: 2) | |
| 3 | Tie 10 of the 12 loose sticks into a bundle. Write 2, and a small 1 above the tens | "इकाई के नीचे एक ही अंक आता है। 12 यानी एक दहाई और 2 इकाई: 2 लिखो, और दहाई ऊपर चली गई — दहाई के ऊपर छोटा सा 1।" | "Only one digit fits under the ones. 12 is one ten and 2 ones: write 2, and the ten goes up — a little 1 on top of the tens." |
| 4 | Point to 3, 2 and the small 1 | "दहाई: 3 और 2, और हासिल का 1। तो 38 जमा 24 कितना?" | "Tens: 3 and 2, and the 1 we carried. So 38 plus 24 is…?" |
| ⏸ **Pause 2** | Look at the camera, 3 s | Answers: **62**, **52** (right: 62) | |
| 5 | Write 6 | "3 और 2 मिलकर 5, और हासिल का 1 जोड़कर 6: 62। हासिल भूल जाते तो 52 होता।" | "3 and 2 is 5, and the carried 1 makes 6: 62. Forgetting the carried ten would give 52." |

## 3. `v-borrow` — Borrowing a ten

**Props:** a slate with **52 − 27** as a column sum, and 5 bundles of ten plus 2 loose sticks.

| # | Show | Say (Hindi) | Say (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The column sum; point to 2 and 7 | "52 में से 27। पहले इकाई: 2 में से 7। 2, 7 से छोटा है। क्या करें?" | "52 take away 27. Ones first: 2 take away 7. 2 is smaller than 7. What do we do?" |
| ⏸ **Pause 1** | Look at the camera, 3 s | Answers: **Borrow a ten / एक दहाई उधार लो** (right), **Do 7 take away 2 / 7 में से 2 कर दो** | |
| 2 | Take the rubber band off one bundle | "ऊपर वाली संख्या से घटाते हैं — 2 से। वह छोटी है, इसलिए एक बंडल खोलते हैं। अब 4 दहाई हैं, और 12 इकाई।" | "We take from the top number — 2. It is too small, so we open a bundle. Now there are 4 tens, and 12 ones." |
| 3 | Cross out 5 → 4 and 2 → 12 on the slate | "12 में से 7 गए, 5 बचे। दहाई: 4 में से 2। तो 52 में से 27 घटाओ तो कितना?" | "12 take away 7 is 5. Tens: 4 take away 2. So 52 take away 27 is…?" |
| ⏸ **Pause 2** | Look at the camera, 3 s | Answers: **35**, **25** (right: 25) | |
| 4 | Write 2 | "उधार देने के बाद 4 ही दहाई बचीं: 4 में से 2 गए, 2 बचे। 25।" | "After borrowing there are 4 tens, not 5: 4 take away 2 is 2. 25." |

## 4. `v-matra` — The ा and ि signs

**Props:** a slate or paper and a thick marker, to write big letters. Film this one mostly in Hindi. The English take explains in English but writes the same Hindi letters.

| # | Show | Say (Hindi) | Say (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Write **क**, then add **ा** | "यह क है। इसके बाद खड़ी पाई ा लगाओ, तो क लंबा हो जाता है: का।" | "This is क. Add the standing line ा after it, and क becomes long: का." |
| 2 | Write **मा** | "म और ा से बना मा। मा… मा… मामा! कौन सा "का" है?" — hold up **क** and **का** | "म and ा make मा. मा… मा… मामा! Which one says "का"?" — hold up **क** and **का** |
| ⏸ **Pause 1** | Look at the camera, 3 s | Answers: **क**, **का** (right: का) | |
| 3 | Point to the line in का | "का में खड़ी पाई है।" | "का has the standing line." |
| 4 | Write **ि** first, then **क** after it | "यह मात्रा ि अक्षर से पहले लिखी जाती है, पर बोली बाद में जाती है: कि।" | "This sign ि is written BEFORE the letter, but we say it after: कि." |
| 5 | Write **दिन**, **किताब**, then show **मि** and **मा** | "दिन। किताब। कौन सा "मि" है?" | "दिन. किताब. Which one says "मि"?" |
| ⏸ **Pause 2** | Look at the camera, 3 s | Answers: **मि**, **मा** (right: मि) | |
| 6 | Point to मि | "मि — मात्रा पहले लिखी है, पर बोलते म पहले हैं।" | "मि — the sign comes first, but we say म first." |

## 5. `v-clock` — What o'clock is it?

**Props:** a real clock whose hands can be turned (a toy clock, or a paper plate with card hands).

| # | Show | Say (Hindi) | Say (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Clock at 3:00; point to the short hand | "घड़ी में दो सुइयाँ होती हैं। छोटी सुई घंटा बताती है।" | "A clock has two hands. The short hand tells the hour." |
| 2 | Point to the long hand at 12 | "बड़ी सुई सीधे ऊपर, 12 पर है। तो 3 बजे हैं।" | "The long hand points straight up, to the 12. So it is 3 o'clock." |
| 3 | Set the clock to 7:00, close up | "इस घड़ी को देखो। कितने बजे हैं?" | "Look at this clock. What time is it?" |
| ⏸ **Pause 1** | Look at the camera, 3 s | Answers: **7 बजे / 7 o'clock** (right), **12 बजे / 12 o'clock** | |
| 4 | Point to the short hand on 7 | "घंटा छोटी सुई बताती है — वह 7 पर है। 7 बजे।" | "The short hand tells the hour — it is on 7. 7 o'clock." |
| 5 | Turn the long hand once round; the short hand moves from 3 to 4 | "बड़ी सुई एक घंटे में पूरा चक्कर लगाती है। छोटी सुई अगली संख्या पर पहुँचती है।" | "The long hand goes all the way round in one hour. The short hand moves to the next number." |
| 6 | Set the clock to 9:00 | "एक और। कितने बजे हैं?" | "One more. What time is it?" |
| ⏸ **Pause 2** | Look at the camera, 3 s | Answers: **10 बजे / 10 o'clock**, **9 बजे / 9 o'clock** (right: 9) | |
| 7 | Point to 9 | "छोटी सुई 9 पर है: 9 बजे।" | "The short hand is on 9: 9 o'clock." |

## After filming: putting a clip in the app

1. **Trim** the start and end, so it starts when the teacher starts talking.
2. **Make it small and phone-friendly.** Keep each file under 25 MB:
   ```bash
   ffmpeg -i take.mov -vf scale=-2:720 -c:v libx264 -preset veryfast -crf 28 \
          -c:a aac -b:a 96k -movflags +faststart video/v-tens-hi.mp4
   ```
   Name each file `video/<video id>-<hi|en>.mp4`. `+faststart` lets it start playing before it has fully downloaded.
3. **Write down the two pause seconds.** Play the trimmed file and note the second at which the teacher finishes each question, for example 41.5 and 96.
4. **List it in `video/clips.json`:**
   ```json
   {
     "clips": {
       "v-tens": {
         "hi": { "src": "video/v-tens-hi.mp4", "pauses": [41.5, 96] },
         "en": { "src": "video/v-tens-en.mp4", "pauses": [39, 92.5] }
       }
     }
   }
   ```
   There must be exactly one pause per question (two for every video here), each later than the one before. Otherwise the app ignores the clip and keeps playing the animated version, so a typo can't break a lesson.
5. **Bump `CACHE` in `sw.js`** and deploy. A phone keeps a clip once it has played it online, and plays it again in airplane mode after that.

## Munni's voice for the animated versions

Run the narration builder once for each language with your Gemini key (`voice/.env` on your own machine). It now includes every line of these five videos and the read-aloud lines for Nursery–Class 2:

```bash
node tools/build-audio.mjs --lang hi
node tools/build-audio.mjs --lang en
```

Until it's run, the animated versions speak with the live voice when there's a signal and stay quiet offline.
